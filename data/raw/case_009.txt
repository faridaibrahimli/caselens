=== GROUND TRUTH (for your eyes only) ===
Outside a bar late at night, two men got into an argument that ended with one
shoving the other, who fell and hit his head on the curb. A third person was
standing nearby but was not involved in the altercation itself.

=== WITNESS 1: Bartender, watched from the doorway ===
I saw the two of them arguing outside for a minute or so before it happened.
The taller guy shoved the other one first, and he fell back onto the curb.
There was another guy standing a bit further off, but he didn't get involved at all.
I called for help right after he fell.

=== WITNESS 2: Person waiting outside for a taxi ===
I saw some pushing but I actually think the shorter guy shoved first, then got shoved back harder.
It happened fast, so I could be wrong about who started it.
I didn't really notice a third person until after the fall, when someone came over to help.
I didn't hear most of what they were arguing about.

=== WITNESS 3: Friend of the man who fell ===
We'd been at the bar together earlier, then went outside for some air.
I saw the shove happen, the other guy definitely pushed first, no question in my mind.
There wasn't anyone else really close to them when it happened, just the two of them.
I ran over right after he hit the ground.

=== WITNESS 4: Passerby across the street ===
I was just walking by and noticed the two of them arguing loudly.
I saw a third person standing pretty close to them the whole time, not just after.
I couldn't tell you who pushed who first, I only glanced over once I heard shouting.
I kept walking after I saw someone go down, I didn't want to get involved.
