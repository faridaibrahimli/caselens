=== GROUND TRUTH (for your eyes only) ===
A package was delivered to a doorstep around 2pm. It was stolen sometime
between 2pm and 4pm, when the homeowner returned and found it gone.
One person took the package, on foot.

=== WITNESS 1: Homeowner ===
I was expecting a delivery that afternoon, tracking said it arrived around 2.
I got home around 4 and the package was gone from the porch.
I didn't see anyone on my camera, it must not have been recording that day.
I called the delivery company first to make sure it wasn't a mistake.

=== WITNESS 2: Neighbor across the street ===
I saw someone walk up to the porch and pick up a box, then walk off down the street.
I'd guess it was around 2:30, I was having a coffee on my porch around then.
It looked like just one person to me, walking, no car involved.
I didn't recognize them, but I wasn't paying too much attention at first.

=== WITNESS 3: Neighbor next door ===
I noticed a car slow down near that house sometime in the afternoon, maybe closer to 3:30.
Two people got out briefly, though I couldn't say if they went up to the porch or not.
They didn't stay long, the car drove off after a couple of minutes.
I didn't think about it again until I heard about the missing package.

=== WITNESS 4: Mail carrier ===
I dropped that package off right around 2, like the tracking said.
Everything was fine when I left it, nothing seemed out of place.
I didn't see anyone else on the street at that time.
I don't recall anything unusual on my route that day otherwise.
