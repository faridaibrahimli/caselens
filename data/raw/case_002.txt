=== GROUND TRUTH (for your eyes only) ===
Sam boards bus 156 at 8:15am, heading to uni (arrival 8:48am).
He runs into a friend from uni and starts chatting near the side door.
At 8:33am, near the side door, a teenage girl in a dark blue sweatshirt
with a large beige bag steals his phone from his jacket pocket.
Sam only notices the phone is missing right before reaching his uni stop.

=== WITNESS 1: Sam (victim) ===
I got on the bus around 8:15 and found a seat near the middle.
A few stops in, I spotted my friend from uni and went over to chat with them by the side door.
We were talking the whole way, so I wasn't really paying attention to who was around us.
I only checked my phone because I wanted to text my mum, and that's when I realized it was gone.
That must have been just a couple of minutes before my stop.

=== WITNESS 2: Passenger seated near the side door ===
I was sitting close to the side door for most of the ride.
There was a girl standing there for a while, dark blue hoodie, carrying a big beige bag.
She got on a few stops before mine and didn't say much to anyone.
I remember her being close to two boys who were chatting near the door.
I got off before the uni stop so I didn't see what happened after that.

=== WITNESS 3: Bus driver ===
It was a normal, fairly busy morning route, nothing unusual that I noticed.
I don't really see much of what happens in the middle of the bus from my seat.
Around that time I was focused on the traffic, there was some congestion near the school.
I don't remember anyone acting suspicious near the doors, but honestly I wouldn't have noticed.

=== WITNESS 4: Passenger standing near the front ===
I was standing near the front for the whole ride, holding onto the rail.
At some point I glanced toward the side door and saw a girl there, but she was wearing a black jacket, not blue.
She had some kind of bag with her, I want to say light-colored, but I'm not totally sure.
Honestly I was reading the new chapter of a manga on my phone most of the ride, so I wasn't paying much attention.

=== WITNESS 5: The girl (later identified, gives a statement) ===
I was on the bus that morning but I stayed near the front the whole time, not by the side door.
I didn't really notice anyone chatting near me, it was a pretty quiet ride.
I don't remember seeing a boy on his phone or anyone who looked upset.
I got off a couple of stops before the university, so whatever happened must have been after I left.

=== WITNESS 6: Passenger who got off earlier ===
I noticed a girl and a boy standing quite close to each other near the side door.
He seemed busy on his phone the whole time, didn't look like they were talking much.
She had a fairly big bag with her, light-colored I think, though I couldn't swear to the exact shade.
I got off a few stops before the university stop, so I don't know what happened after that.
