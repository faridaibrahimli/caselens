=== GROUND TRUTH (for your eyes only) ===
Juli met her friends at a cafe in the city center from 1pm to 5pm.
A man in a grey jacket stole a bicycle from outside the cafe at 3pm.

=== WITNESS 1: Cafe customer ===
I was busy talking with my friends and didn't pay much attention to what was happening outside.
I do remember that when I looked out the window around 3:30pm, there was no bicycle outside anymore.
I couldn't say exactly when it disappeared.

=== WITNESS 2: Passerby ===
I was on my break from work, walking near the cafe between 2:30pm and 3pm.
I noticed a person in a black jacket standing close to the bicycles outside.
I didn't think much of it at the time since people stop there all the time.

=== WITNESS 3: Waitress at the cafe ===
I was serving tables near the window around 3pm.
I remember a man standing outside for a little while, close to where the bikes were parked.
I was busy and didn't get a good look at his face or what he was wearing.

=== WITNESS 4: Mike, Juli's friend ===
I stepped outside for a bit and saw a person near the bicycles.
He was wearing a grey jacket, I'm fairly sure about that.
I don't remember the exact time, but it felt closer to 4pm than 3pm.
