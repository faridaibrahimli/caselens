=== GROUND TRUTH (for your eyes only) ===
After the football match, a clash broke out between rival fan groups near the metro station, at night.
One fan (the victim, Team A) was struck with a bottle and taken to hospital with a head injury.
The bottle brand was later identified; it is not commonly sold or carried by fans of either team specifically,
so brand alone does not settle which side it belonged to.
It was dark, chaotic, and several witnesses had been drinking.

=== WITNESS 1: The victim (Team A) ===
I remember the shouting starting, and then everyone was pushing toward us.
I didn't see what hit me, it just happened, and then I was on the ground.
I couldn't tell you who threw anything, my head was already spinning before I even understood what happened.
I don't really remember much clearly after that, it's a bit of a blur.

=== WITNESS 2: Team A supporter, victim's friend ===
We were walking toward the metro after the match when a group of Team B fans started shouting at us.
It got out of hand fast, people were pushing, and then I saw my friend go down.
I think the bottle came from someone in the Team B group, but everyone was moving around so much.
Our side definitely doesn't drink that brand, I'm sure of that much at least.

=== WITNESS 3: Team A supporter, further back ===
I was a bit behind when it happened, so I only saw the aftermath, people shouting and my friend on the ground.
I didn't see the throw myself, someone just said it came from the other side.
I personally don't drink that brand, but I couldn't really speak for everyone else in our group.
It was dark and everyone had scarves and hats on, hard to make out faces.

=== WITNESS 4: Team B supporter ===
Things got heated on both sides, people were shouting insults back and forth.
I didn't see anyone from our side throw a bottle, if anything it looked like it came from within the Team A crowd.
Our group doesn't really drink that brand, we were mostly drinking something else that night.
It was chaotic and dark, so I wouldn't want to accuse anyone specifically.

=== WITNESS 5: Team B supporter, near the front ===
I was close to the front when the pushing started.
I actually saw the bottle in someone's hand right before it was thrown, a guy in a grey Team A scarf.
It happened fast but I'm fairly confident about that part, it stuck with me.
I don't know his name, just what he looked like in that moment.

=== WITNESS 6: Team B supporter, standing to the side ===
I wasn't right in the middle of it, I was a bit off to the side with a few others.
From where I stood, I couldn't tell who threw the bottle, it could have come from either group honestly.
I remember hearing it break rather than seeing it thrown.
People were already drinking a lot before this happened, so I'd say everyone's a little unreliable here, us included.
