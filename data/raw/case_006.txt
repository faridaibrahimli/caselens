=== GROUND TRUTH (for your eyes only) ===
A car parked on Level 2, Row C of the downtown parking garage was broken into
sometime between 6pm and 8pm. A window was smashed and a bag was taken from
the back seat. A man in a dark hoodie was seen near the car shortly before
the theft was discovered.

=== WITNESS 1: Owner of the car ===
I parked on Level 2 around 5:30pm and went to a dinner nearby.
When I came back around 8pm, the back window was smashed and my bag was gone.
I didn't see anyone unusual when I parked, everything seemed normal.
I called the police right after I noticed the damage.

=== WITNESS 2: Parking attendant ===
I noticed a man in a dark hoodie walking around Level 2 sometime that evening.
He was near Row C, close to where I later heard the car had been broken into.
I'd guess it was around 7, maybe a bit before, but I wasn't watching the clock.
I didn't think much of it at the time, people walk around looking for their cars all the time.

=== WITNESS 3: Driver parked nearby, same level ===
I was loading my own car around 6:45 or so, on Level 2 as well.
I did notice someone in a hoodie, but I actually think he was over on Row A, not Row C.
He wasn't doing anything that caught my attention, just walking.
I left shortly after that, so I don't know what happened afterward.

=== WITNESS 4: Security guard, ground floor ===
I do rounds through the garage a few times an evening, including Level 2.
On my round around 7:30, I didn't notice anything broken or out of place on Row C.
It gets pretty dim up there in the evening, so smaller damage might be easy to miss on a quick pass.
I didn't see anyone matching that description during my round.
