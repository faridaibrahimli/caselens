Case: case_003

Conflict 1
Type: attribution
Witnesses: 2 and 3 (Team A) vs 5 (Team B)
Witnesses 2 and 3 say: the bottle "came from someone in the Team B group" / "someone just said it came from the other side"
Witness 5 says: "I actually saw the bottle in someone's hand right before it was thrown, a guy in a grey Team A scarf"
Note: this is the sharpest conflict in the case — it's not a detail mismatch but a disagreement over which side the attacker belonged to. Witnesses 2 and 3 did not directly see the throw (secondhand/assumed); Witness 5 claims direct, confident observation. Flag as high-priority for investigator review.

Conflict 2
Type: brand/identity (indirect)
Witnesses: 2 vs 5
Witness 2 says: "our side definitely doesn't drink that brand, I'm sure of that much at least"
Witness 5 says: the attacker (in a Team A scarf) was seen holding the bottle
Note: if Witness 5's identification is correct, it directly undercuts Witness 2's claim that the brand isn't associated with Team A. Indirect conflict — arises from combining two statements rather than a single parallel clash. Flag as related to Conflict 1, not fully independent.

Consistent 1 (not a conflict)
Type: certainty hedge
Witnesses: 3 vs 4
Witness 3 says: "I personally don't drink that brand, but I couldn't really speak for everyone else in our group"
Witness 4 says: "our group doesn't really drink that brand"
Note: Witness 3's hedge is weaker and does not actually conflict with Witness 4's claim (different teams, both denying the brand for themselves). Included as a negative example — should NOT be flagged as a contradiction, despite surface similarity in topic.

Unreliable/low-value testimony (context, not a formal conflict)
Witness 1 (victim): no usable identification due to injury and shock — flag as low-confidence source rather than conflicting source.
Witness 6: explicitly states uncertainty on both sides ("could have come from either group") — useful as a neutral, self-aware source, corroborates the general unreliability rather than pointing blame anywhere.

Ground truth (not given to model): victim (Team A) struck with a bottle near the metro station after the match; brand does not conclusively belong to either side; attacker's identity/team affiliation remains contested between witness accounts.
