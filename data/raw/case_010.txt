=== GROUND TRUTH (for your eyes only) ===
A person was seen shoplifting from a small shop, taking a few items and leaving
without paying, sometime in the early afternoon. They left on foot, alone.

=== WITNESS 1: Shop employee, behind the counter ===
I noticed someone acting a bit off near the shelves, taking a while browsing without picking anything up.
Then I saw them slip a couple of items into their jacket pocket and head for the door.
It was just one person, a young man wearing a navy blue cap.
I didn't manage to stop him before he left.

=== WITNESS 2: Customer inside the shop ===
I was in the next aisle and saw someone put something in their bag quickly.
It looked like just one guy, no one else with him that I noticed.
He had some kind of dark cap on, hard to say the exact color, maybe close to black, but I couldn't really tell in that lighting.
I didn't think much of it until I heard the employee shout after him.

=== WITNESS 3: Passerby outside the shop window ===
I was walking past and glanced in through the window right as it happened.
I saw someone grab something off a shelf and walk out quickly after.
I'm pretty sure there were two of them, one going in the door as the other came out.
I didn't get a good look at either of their faces through the glass.

=== WITNESS 4: Shop owner, arrived shortly after ===
I got back to the shop a few minutes after it happened, my employee told me what happened.
From the description, it sounded like a young man, alone, wearing a cap.
Nothing else seemed to be missing from the shelves as far as I could tell.
I checked the till and it was untouched, so it seems like it was just the items taken.
