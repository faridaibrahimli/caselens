=== GROUND TRUTH (for your eyes only) ===
An important signed contract was kept in the main accountant Anna's office cabinet.
Anna confirmed it was there Monday evening around 6pm, before locking up and leaving.
She discovered it missing Wednesday morning around 9am.
Only 5 people have access to her office: Anna herself, Tom (assistant accountant),
Elena (IT technician), David (office manager), and Marta (the cleaner).

Anna was in the office briefly Tuesday too: she left mid-afternoon for an external
meeting, then returned afterward for about ten minutes, during which she spoke with
Tom about the contract — she was unhappy with some of its terms and said so during
that conversation. She then left again.

Elena's usual work is on a different floor. She states she came to the accounting
floor only once, in the evening, to fix a server issue. In reality, she was there
twice — once in the afternoon with no clear work reason, and again in the evening,
when she took the document. Her account undercounts her own visits.

David told everyone he left the office at a normal time that evening. In truth, he
stayed much later than he claims — but not because of the document. He was having
an affair and stayed late to meet the other person privately in the building, and
lied about his departure time to avoid that becoming known. He did not take the
document and has no knowledge of its disappearance.

=== WITNESS 1: Anna, main accountant ===
I locked the document in my cabinet Monday evening before I left, around 6pm, like always.
I had a meeting outside the office Tuesday afternoon, so I wasn't around much that day.
I did stop by briefly afterward, just for a few minutes, to grab something I'd forgotten.
When I came in Wednesday morning around 9, I opened the cabinet and the document was gone.

=== WITNESS 2: Tom, assistant accountant ===
I was at my desk most of Monday and Tuesday, finishing month-end reports.
Anna came by briefly Tuesday evening, we talked for a few minutes about the contract.
She wasn't happy with some of the terms in it, she said as much to me directly.
I saw David leave around his usual time that evening, nothing seemed unusual to me.

=== WITNESS 3: Elena, IT technician ===
I came in Tuesday evening, around 7 to 9pm, to fix an issue with the office server.
The router is in Anna's office, so I needed access to go in and check the connections.
It took a while because the issue was a bit tricky to trace.
That was the only time I was up on that floor that day.

=== WITNESS 4: David, office manager ===
I saw Anna on the floor a couple of times Tuesday, once in the morning and again later after she got back from her meeting.
I also saw Elena on our floor twice that day, which struck me as odd since she's not usually up here, she's normally downstairs with IT.
I left the office at my usual time that evening, nothing out of the ordinary on my end.
I don't know anything about the document going missing.

=== WITNESS 5: Marta, cleaner ===
I do a light tidy during the day and then a full clean in the evening, usually starting around 8 and finishing by 9:30.
During the day I saw a woman near Anna's office, but I couldn't say for sure if it was Anna or Elena, I only caught a glimpse.
In the evening, Anna's office was locked and dark when I got to it, same as most evenings.
David's car was still in the parking lot quite a bit later than I'd expect, well after when I'd normally see him gone.
