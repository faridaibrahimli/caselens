=== GROUND TRUTH (for your eyes only) ===
Sometime overnight, a shop's metal shutter was spray-painted with graffiti.
The shop owner discovered it the next morning around 7am when opening up.
No alarm was triggered; the act happened quietly, late at night, sometime
after 12:45am (the graffiti was not yet there when the food stall worker
passed by at that time).

=== WITNESS 1: Shop owner ===
I closed up as usual around 9pm the night before, everything was fine then.
When I came back this morning around 7, the shutter was covered in graffiti.
I didn't hear anything unusual overnight, I live a few streets away.
Nothing inside the shop was touched, just the outside shutter.

=== WITNESS 2: Resident living above the shop ===
I heard some noise late at night, around 12:30, like something being dragged or shaken.
Right after that, I heard spraying sounds, it went on for a couple of minutes.
I looked out my window right then and the shutter already had fresh paint on it.
I didn't see who was doing it, just heard it and saw the result.

=== WITNESS 3: Passerby, early morning jogger ===
I jog past that street most mornings around 6.
I didn't notice anyone hanging around near the shop when I passed, it was empty.
I do remember seeing the graffiti already there when I passed, though, so it must have happened before 6.
I didn't think much of it at the time, just kept going.

=== WITNESS 4: Late-night food stall worker, corner of the street ===
I was packing up my stall until about 12:45 that night, just down the street.
I walked right past the shop on my way home around then.
The shutter was completely clean at that point, no graffiti at all.
Nothing seemed unusual, and I didn't see or hear anyone around.
