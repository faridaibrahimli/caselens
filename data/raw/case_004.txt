=== GROUND TRUTH (for your eyes only) ===
Tod (11) finished classes at 3pm. On the way home, he stopped at Marcus's house
to look at a new video game and lost track of time, then continued toward the shop
and afterward to the park, where he watched some boys playing football and lost
further track of time, before finally heading home.
He was not in any danger; he simply arrived much later than usual (around 5:15pm).
His mother began worrying at 4:30pm and started calling his friends;
an active search began at 5pm.

Note on Witness 4 (Mr. Petrov): he did see a boy of similar age walking briskly down
Elm Street around 4:45pm, heading home — but it was a different boy entirely, not Tod.
Mr. Petrov's account is accurate about what he saw; the conflict arises from mistaken
identity, not dishonesty or unreliability on his part.

=== WITNESS 1: Ben, classmate ===
We left school together around 3, like always.
He said he wanted to stop by Marcus's house for a bit to see his new game.
I didn't go with him, I went straight home.
That's the last time I saw him that day.

=== WITNESS 2: Marcus, classmate ===
Tod came by for maybe twenty minutes, we looked at my new game together.
He left my place and said he was heading straight home after.
I think it was around 3:30 when he left, maybe a bit later.
I didn't see which way he went after he left my street.

=== WITNESS 3: Mrs. Alvarez, shopkeeper ===
I saw him come into the shop that afternoon, he bought a small snack.
He seemed in a normal mood, nothing seemed wrong.
I'd say it was closer to 4:15 when he was here, we were fairly busy around then.
He left heading toward the park, not toward his usual street.

=== WITNESS 4: Neighbor, Mr. Petrov ===
I saw him walking near the corner of Elm Street, by himself.
He looked like he was in a hurry, walking fast.
It must have been around 4:45, I remember because I was taking the bins out.
He went straight down Elm Street, toward his house.

=== WITNESS 5: Neighbor, Mrs. Kim ===
I saw a boy matching his description near the park entrance, not on Elm Street.
He was walking slowly, not in a hurry at all, maybe even a bit distracted.
I think it was around 4:30, but I wasn't looking at the clock.
I didn't see where he went after that, I went back inside.

=== WITNESS 6: Taxi driver ===
I was parked near the shop for a break around that time.
I noticed a boy leave the shop and go toward the park side, not toward the houses.
I couldn't say the exact time, maybe half four, things blur together on a long shift.
I didn't see him again after that.
